# "What If" Concepts — 30-second stick-figure shorts

Each script is about 75–85 words, which runs about 30 seconds of narration.
**Hook** is the first 2 seconds of on-screen text. **Visuals** are stick-figure beats.
The facts follow mainstream science; the scenarios are hypothetical.

---

## 1. What if you could stop time for everyone except you?
**Hook:** "You freeze time. Bad news: you're about to die."

**Script:**
You snap your fingers and the whole world freezes. Free money, right? Wrong. If time stops, air molecules stop too. Every breath feels like pushing against concrete. Light stops moving, so the world goes pitch black. Sound can't travel. You'd be blind, deaf, and suffocating inside a world of frozen air. Your superpower is actually a coffin. So next time you wish you could stop time… maybe just ask for five more minutes of sleep.

**Visuals:** stick figure snaps → everyone frozen mid-step → stick figure tries to walk, air shown as a brick-texture wall → screen fades black → figure in a coffin-shaped frame → alarm clock gag.

---

## 2. What if the Moon was replaced by a black hole of the same mass?
**Hook:** "Swap the Moon for a black hole. Nothing happens… at first."

**Script:**
Replace the Moon with a black hole of the exact same mass, and here's the twist: the tides don't change. Gravity only cares about mass, not size. The black hole would be smaller than a grain of sand. But the nights? Pitch black. No moonlight, ever again. Nocturnal animals would lose their guide. Sea turtles would get lost. And astronomers would be staring at an invisible dot that bends starlight around it like a cosmic magnifying glass.

**Visuals:** Moon swapped for a tiny dot → waves unchanged with a thumbs-up figure → lights out → confused turtle stick figure → telescope figure sees warped stars.

---

## 3. What if humans could photosynthesize?
**Hook:** "You never have to eat again. There's a catch."

**Script:**
Imagine your skin could make food from sunlight like a plant. No more groceries. But here's the problem: a human has only about two square meters of skin. To power your body, you'd need the surface area of a tennis court. So you'd have to be enormous, flat, and green, and you'd stand still in a field all day. Congratulations. You've invented a tree.

**Visuals:** green stick figure sunbathing → "2 m²" label vs. a tennis court → figure stretched into a huge flat sheet → slowly morphs into a tree → tree blinks.

---

## 4. What if you fell into a hole through the entire Earth?
**Hook:** "Jump into a hole through the Earth. You'll never stop."

**Script:**
Dig a tunnel straight through Earth and jump in. You'd accelerate to nearly eight kilometers per second and pass through the core in about nineteen minutes. Then you'd slow down and pop out on the other side, just barely reaching the surface. Then you'd fall back. Forever. Back and forth, every 42 minutes, like a human pendulum. Unless air resistance stops you in the middle. Then you're stuck at the center of the Earth. Floating. Weightless. Alone.

**Visuals:** cutaway Earth with a tunnel → figure falls with a speed counter → bounces back and forth with a "42 min" timer → slows → floats at the core with a sad face.

---

## 5. What if everyone on Earth disappeared except you?
**Hook:** "Everyone vanished. Day 1 is fun. Day 2 is not."

**Script:**
Day one: free pizza, empty roads, every mansion is yours. Within hours, power plants without operators start shutting down. Within days, the lights go out everywhere. Week one, the internet dies. Month one, the food rots. A few years later, vines crack the highways. And you? You're the only human voice on a silent planet. Turns out the scariest sound in the world isn't a scream. It's nothing.

**Visuals:** figure partying in an empty city → city grid blinking off → Wi-Fi icon crossed out → calendar flipping → plants overrunning roads → lone figure on a rooftop, total silence (cut the music).

---

## 6. What if Earth had rings like Saturn?
**Hook:** "Earth gets rings. Your view changes forever."

**Script:**
If Earth had rings, they'd stretch across the sky like a glowing bridge from horizon to horizon. Near the equator you'd see them edge-on, a razor-thin line. Farther north they'd be a giant arch overhead. But the rings would cast enormous shadows, making some regions permanently colder. And every so often, chunks would fall, so meteor showers would be daily. The view? Incredible. The weather forecast? Terrifying.

**Visuals:** stick figure looks up at a ring arch → split screen equator vs. north → shadow band freezing a town → rocks falling → news anchor stick figure sweating.

---

## 7. What if you could only tell the truth for 24 hours?
**Hook:** "Truth serum. 24 hours. Your boss calls."

**Script:**
For 24 hours, every word out of your mouth is true. Your mom asks if you like her cooking. Your friend shows you their haircut. Your boss asks why you're late. By lunch, you've lost two friends and a job. But something strange happens. People start trusting you completely. Studies suggest most of us tell one or two lies a day. Without them, you'd be the most hated and most trusted person in town.

**Visuals:** figure with a glowing "TRUTH" mouth → quick-cut reactions (mom shocked, friend crying, boss pointing out) → crowd slowly turns to nod → figure holds a "trust" meter at 100%.

---

## 8. What if the Sun disappeared?
**Hook:** "The Sun vanishes. You won't notice for 8 minutes."

**Script:**
If the Sun vanished right now, you wouldn't know. Light takes eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach us, and so does gravity's change. Then darkness. Earth flies off in a straight line into space. Within a week, the surface drops below freezing. Within a year, the oceans' surface ices over. But deep down, near volcanic vents, life could survive in total darkness, warmed by the Earth itself.

**Visuals:** Sun pops out → countdown "8:20" → figure still sunbathing → lights out → Earth drifting off on a dotted line → ice spreading → tiny glowing creatures by a vent.

---

## 9. What if you swallowed a tiny black hole?
**Hook:** "You swallowed a black hole. Here's what happens."

**Script:**
A black hole with the mass of a mountain would be smaller than an atom. Swallow it, and it would fall right through you, then through the floor, then into Earth. It wouldn't even slow down. It would just orbit back and forth through the planet, quietly nibbling. But a black hole that tiny is also blazingly hot. It would blast out radiation like a nuclear reactor. So you'd never get to swallow it. You'd be vaporized just by being near it.

**Visuals:** figure about to gulp a glowing dot → dot falls through the figure, floor, Earth → orbit loop → dot glows white-hot → figure becomes a pile of ash wearing sunglasses.

---

## 10. What if gravity turned off for 5 seconds?
**Hook:** "Gravity off. 5 seconds. Total chaos."

**Script:**
Five seconds without gravity. Everything not tied down starts drifting. You float off your chair. Cars lift off the road. The oceans bulge. The atmosphere starts to expand outward. Then gravity snaps back on. Everything slams down at once. Planes in flight are fine, but billions of people fall from a few centimeters to a few meters. Phones crack everywhere. And somewhere, an astronaut on the space station is wondering what all the fuss is about.

**Visuals:** big "5" countdown → figures, cars and a cat floating → water bulge → SLAM → cracked phone icons everywhere → astronaut stick figure shrugging.
