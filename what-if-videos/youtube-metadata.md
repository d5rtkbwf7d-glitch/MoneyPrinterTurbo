- **Title:** under 70 characters so it isn't cut off.
- **Description:** paste as-is. The first 3 hashtags show above the title.
- **Tags:** paste into the Tags field. Each list is under YouTube's 500-character limit.

## Upload tips
- **Hashtags:** keep 3 to 5 per description. The first three show above the title, and more than 15 makes YouTube ignore all of them.
- **First line:** your hook. It's what viewers see before tapping "more".
- **Pinned comment:** ask the video's closing question ("Hot side or cold side?") as your own pinned comment. It gets comments going early.
- **Long-form:** the two long videos (`longform-demographics/`, `longform-first-home/`) are regular uploads, not Shorts. Tell me if you want metadata for those too, with chapters/timestamps.
